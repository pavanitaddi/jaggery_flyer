# 🌿 G ONE JAGGERY FOODS – Organic Product Promotional Flyer

## 📌 Project Overview

G ONE JAGGERY FOODS is a creative digital promotional flyer designed to showcase traditional Indian organic food products and festival special offers.

The flyer highlights different product packages, their ingredients, pricing, and contact information through an attractive and user-friendly visual design.

The main objective of this project is to promote organic products and attract customers through professional digital advertising.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Create an attractive promotional flyer for an organic food business.
- Showcase different product packages and their prices.
- Highlight traditional Indian food products.
- Promote natural and preservative-free products.
- Provide customers with clear ordering and contact information.
